Founder's office work in health and D2C food

I work as Chief of Staff to Revant Himatsingka at Food Pharmer and as Senior Manager, Creative & Content Strategy at Only What's Needed. My role includes label checks, data analysis, and supporting product decisions.

What the work covers

  • Checking labels and claims. I check labels and claims before anything goes out, and get products ready for FSSAI's proposed warning labels.
  • Data analysis. I use open data like the India Label Index to support product decisions and strategy.
  • Creative and content strategy. I work on creative and content strategy at Only What's Needed.
  • Founder's priorities and launches. I work on the founder's priorities, launches and the weekly numbers.

What I have done

  • Senior Manager, Creative & Content Strategy, Only What's Needed (OWN).
  • Chief of Staff to Revant Himatsingka at Food Pharmer, and founding team at Only What's Needed (OWN).
  • Built the India Label Index: open data on 3,798 Indian packaged-food ingredient lists. 6 in 10 list an added sugar.
  • Ran FSSAI's proposed warning limits on 665 foods: 71% would carry a warning under its current plan, against 41% under the plan it dropped.
  • Built the free Label Checker: paste a label, scan a barcode or snap a photo.
  • Studied 900 LinkedIn posts from 9 health voices to learn what makes people act on health advice.
  • Before that: Indus Insights (analytics), Grant Thornton Bharat (NHAI team), Sploot (founder's office). SRCC, Delhi.
