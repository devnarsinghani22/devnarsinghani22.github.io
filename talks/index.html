Dev Narsinghani's talks on food labels and health

I make honest food easy to choose, and I build my own AI to do it. I speak about food labels and health.

Talks

Reading an Indian food label in 10 seconds

You learn to check added sugar, salt, and fat in 10 seconds. You also learn to spot sugar hiding in the first three ingredients.

What Indian food labels actually show

You see that 6 in 10 of the 3,798 ingredient lists name an added sugar. You also see that 71% of 665 foods would carry a warning under FSSAI's current plan.

Building my own AI as a non-engineer

You learn how I run AI models on my laptop for marketing work. It's a talk for students and marketers who want to use AI.

Sleep and the snack aisle

You learn why tired people pick the worst packs. When you are tired, you reach for exactly the packs I warn people about.

Who they are for

  • College fests and TEDx. Students learn to read any pack in 10 seconds and make better choices.
  • Companies. Teams learn about food labels and how to make better wellness choices.
  • Schools and parents. Parents learn how to read labels and make better choices for their kids.

Formats

  • A short talk on food labels and health.
  • A longer workshop where people read real packs they bring.
  • A panel on health, food, and AI.
