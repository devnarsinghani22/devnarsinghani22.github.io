Quick answers

How many Indian chips and crisps have added sugar?

66% of the 163 chips and crisps labels I read list an added sugar. In 16% a sugar is one of the first 3 ingredients, so it is a big part of the food.

Do chips and crisps hide sugar under other names?

12% list sugar under 2 or more names. After plain sugar, the most common is dextrose, on 7% of labels.

How much sugar is in Indian chips and crisps?

The median is 2.5 g of sugar in 100 g, about 0.6 teaspoons. That is from 60 products with a sugar value.

How much salt is in Indian chips and crisps?

The median is 1.69 g of salt in 100 g. That is 34% of the 5 g a day WHO sets for adults. From 58 products with a salt or sodium value.

How to check a pack yourself

Turn the pack over. Read the list at the back from the top, and look for every name of sugar, not just the word sugar. Then check the sugar and salt for 100 g in the nutrition table. Paste any list into the Label Checker and it marks the sugar names for you.
