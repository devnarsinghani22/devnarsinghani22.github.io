Quick answers

How many Indian chocolate and confectionery have added sugar?

86% of the 236 chocolate and confectionery labels I read list an added sugar. In 77% a sugar is one of the first 3 ingredients, so it is a big part of the food.

Do chocolate and confectionery hide sugar under other names?

34% list sugar under 2 or more names. After plain sugar, the most common is liquid glucose, on 25% of labels.

How much sugar is in Indian chocolate and confectionery?

The median is 46.33 g of sugar in 100 g, about 12 teaspoons. That is from 82 products with a sugar value.

How much salt is in Indian chocolate and confectionery?

The median is 0.24 g of salt in 100 g. That is 5% of the 5 g a day WHO sets for adults. From 77 products with a salt or sodium value.

How to check a pack yourself

Turn the pack over. Read the list at the back from the top, and look for every name of sugar, not just the word sugar. Then check the sugar and salt for 100 g in the nutrition table. Paste any list into the Label Checker and it marks the sugar names for you.
