Quick answers

How many food samples fail in India?

In 2025-26, 17.9% of samples failed, which is 40,023 of 223,808. This is down from 28.6% in 2018-19.

Which states fail the most and why that is not a simple ranking?

Uttar Pradesh had the highest fail share over 2021-22 to 2025-26, with 56.3% of its samples failing. A high fail share can mean more bad food or more targeted sampling, and a low one can mean clean food or light testing.

Which states test the least per head?

In 2021-22 to 2025-26, Jharkhand tested 0.19 samples per 10,000 residents a year on average, the lowest of the big states, then Bihar 0.21.

What happens after a sample fails?

Over five years, 75.1 civil penalties and 3.3 convictions were given for every 100 failed samples. In 2025-26, penalties raised Rs 114.81 crore.

How many failures are label mistakes?

Over 2019-20 to 2023-24, 30.0% of failures were label mistakes or misleading claims. In 2023-24, this share dropped to 13.1%, but the Ministry did not explain why.

How I did it

The numbers come from replies by the Health Ministry in Parliament and FSSAI annual reports. The samples are picked by food safety officers, not a random sample of all food on sale. Lab and officer capacity differs by state, so testing rates vary. A failed sample can be a label mistake, not only unsafe food. The 2025-26 numbers are provisional and may change. Population is based on the 2011 Census. No brands are named in the data. Convictions in a year can come from samples taken in earlier years, so the conviction figure is a rough ratio, not a case-by-case follow-up.

What this can't tell you

  • These are not random samples but targeted by food safety officers, and lab and officer capacity varies by state.
  • A failed sample may be a label error, not necessarily unsafe food.
  • Population data is from the 2011 Census.
  • Convictions in a year can come from samples taken in earlier years, making the ratio a rough estimate.
  • The 2025-26 numbers are provisional and may change.
