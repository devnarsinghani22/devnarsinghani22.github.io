Quick answers

How many Indian namkeen and savoury snacks have added sugar?

52% of the 105 namkeen and savoury snacks labels I read list an added sugar. In 10% a sugar is one of the first 3 ingredients, so it is a big part of the food.

Do namkeen and savoury snacks hide sugar under other names?

7% list sugar under 2 or more names. After plain sugar, the most common is dextrose, on 3% of labels.

How much sugar is in Indian namkeen and savoury snacks?

The median is 3.4 g of sugar in 100 g, about 0.8 teaspoons. That is from 30 products with a sugar value.

How to check a pack yourself

Turn the pack over. Read the list at the back from the top, and look for every name of sugar, not just the word sugar. Then check the sugar and salt for 100 g in the nutrition table. Paste any list into the Label Checker and it marks the sugar names for you.
