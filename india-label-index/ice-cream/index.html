Quick answers

How many Indian ice cream and frozen desserts have added sugar?

87% of the 106 ice cream and frozen desserts labels I read list an added sugar. In 71% a sugar is one of the first 3 ingredients, so it is a big part of the food.

Do ice cream and frozen desserts hide sugar under other names?

43% list sugar under 2 or more names. After plain sugar, the most common is liquid glucose, on 34% of labels.

How to check a pack yourself

Turn the pack over. Read the list at the back from the top, and look for every name of sugar, not just the word sugar. Then check the sugar and salt for 100 g in the nutrition table. Paste any list into the Label Checker and it marks the sugar names for you.
