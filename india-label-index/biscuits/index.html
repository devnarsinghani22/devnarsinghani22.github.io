Quick answers

How many Indian biscuits and cookies have added sugar?

93% of the 344 biscuits and cookies labels I read list an added sugar. In 80% a sugar is one of the first 3 ingredients, so it is a big part of the food.

Do biscuits and cookies hide sugar under other names?

70% list sugar under 2 or more names. After plain sugar, the most common is invert sugar, on 49% of labels.

How much sugar is in Indian biscuits and cookies?

The median is 25 g of sugar in 100 g, about 6 teaspoons. That is from 96 products with a sugar value.

How much salt is in Indian biscuits and cookies?

The median is 0.57 g of salt in 100 g. That is 11% of the 5 g a day WHO sets for adults. From 90 products with a salt or sodium value.

How to check a pack yourself

Turn the pack over. Read the list at the back from the top, and look for every name of sugar, not just the word sugar. Then check the sugar and salt for 100 g in the nutrition table. Paste any list into the Label Checker and it marks the sugar names for you.

Where this comes from
